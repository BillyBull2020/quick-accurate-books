The Quick Accurate Books Proactive Bookkeeping System

Tanya Frank's signature approach: weekly bookkeeping that catches problems before they become expensive. Instead of reactive year-end scrambling, the system maintains real-time financial visibility so business owners always know exactly where they stand.

The 5-Step Process

  1. Step 1: Weekly Transaction Categorization — Every transaction classified within 7 days, not at tax time
  2. Step 2: Monthly Reconciliation — Bank and credit card statements matched monthly, no drift
  3. Step 3: Quarterly Tax Projection — Real-time tax liability tracking so there are no surprises
  4. Step 4: Cash Flow Dashboard — Custom dashboard showing 13-week rolling cash flow forecast
  5. Step 5: Annual Financial Health Audit — Comprehensive review of every account, category, and trend

Real Results: A no per-mile cost tracking Case Study

A trucking company with 6 trucks couldn't tell which routes were profitable. We implemented per-mile cost tracking, allocated fuel and maintenance to specific routes, and built a route profitability dashboard. Two routes were operating at a loss after fully loaded costs. The owner renegotiated those contracts or dropped them, saving $27,000/year.

Problem: No per-mile cost tracking, fuel and maintenance costs not allocated to routes
Solution: Per-mile cost accounting + route profitability analysis + IFTA optimization
