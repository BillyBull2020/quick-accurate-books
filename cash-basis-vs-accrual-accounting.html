Cash Basis vs. Accrual Accounting: What’s Best for You?

By Ironclaw Intelligence Engine | Quick Accurate Books

Choosing between cash and accrual accounting fundamentally changes how you view your business’s health. Cash basis is simple: you record income when it hits the bank, and expenses when they leave.

Accrual accounting, however, records revenue when it’s earned (even if you haven't been paid yet). While more complex, accrual accounting provides a far more accurate picture of your long-term profitability.
