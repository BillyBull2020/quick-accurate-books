Phase I: Diagnostic & Data Ingress — The Uncompromised Data Capture

The initial phase of our 44-Point Structural Audit is an intensive, uncompromising data capture and diagnostic sweep. We don’t assume; we verify. This phase lays the groundwork for absolute financial clarity. It’s where every transaction, every ledger entry, every financial document is pulled into our analytical matrix, meticulously cross-referenced and validated.

  • General Ledger Reconciliation (Points 1-5): Comprehensive review of all GL accounts. We're identifying discrepancies, ensuring accurate categorization, and verifying balance sheet integrity down to the penny. No stone left unturned.
  • Bank & Credit Card Reconciliations (Points 6-10): Month-by-month, account-by-account. We're eliminating outstanding items, flagging unauthorized transactions, and establishing a bulletproof match between your records and financial institutions.
  • Accounts Receivable Audit (Points 11-15): Scrutiny of aging reports, unapplied payments, credit policies, and collection procedures. We’re identifying bottlenecks that impede cash flow and proposing aggressive optimization strategies.
  • Accounts Payable Audit (Points 16-20): Examination of vendor invoices, payment terms, expense categorization, and duplicate payment detection. We’re not just paying bills; we’re optimizing your outflow, leveraging every available discount.
  • Payroll System Integrity (Points 21-25): Verification of payroll entries, tax withholdings, benefits deductions, and compliance with federal and state labor laws specific to Colorado. We ensure your payroll isn't a liability, but an asset in legal standing.

This foundational phase is critical. It’s where we identify the latent errors and systemic inconsistencies that silently bleed your business of profit. We're building a real-time, clean data lake, ready for sophisticated analysis.

Phase II: Operational Integrity & Compliance — Fortifying Your Financial Perimeter

Once the raw data is pristine, our focus shifts to operational efficiency and unwavering compliance. This phase ensures your internal processes are robust, secure, and compliant with the labyrinthine regulations governing small businesses, especially in a dynamic market like Thornton. We’re not just preventing problems; we're building a financial fortress.

  • Expense Management Protocol (Points 26-30): Evaluation of expense tracking, approval workflows, and employee reimbursement processes. Are you maximizing deductible expenses? Are you preventing fraud? We’ll tell you.
  • Revenue Recognition Standards (Points 31-35): Reviewing how and when revenue is recognized. This is crucial for accurate financial reporting and tax planning, ensuring you’re compliant with GAAP or relevant industry standards.
  • Fixed Asset & Depreciation Schedule (Points 36-40): Verification of fixed asset registers, depreciation calculations, and appropriate accounting treatment. Incorrect depreciation can lead to significant tax inefficiencies.
  • Inventory Valuation (Points 41-44 for relevant businesses): For product-based businesses, this includes assessing inventory counting methods, valuation techniques (FIFO, LIFO, Weighted Average), and obsolescence provisions. Accuracy here directly impacts COGS and profitability.
  • Tax Readiness & Compliance (Points 45-50): This extends the audit to pre-emptively prepare for local, state, and federal tax obligations. We review sales tax remittance, payroll tax compliance, and income tax estimations. Are you ready for audit season? QAB ensures you always are.

This phase is about more than just avoiding penalties; it’s about optimizing every financial lever within your operation. It’s about establishing processes that are not only compliant but also contribute directly to your bottom line. We’re eliminating the 'soft targets' in your financial operations.
