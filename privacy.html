Commitment to Confidentiality

At Quick Accurate Books, we recognize that your financial data is highly sensitive. We are committed to maintaining the highest standards of privacy and security to protect your business information.

Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to provide our bookkeeping services, including:

  • Contact information (Name, Email, Phone Number)
  • Business financial data provided via secure QuickBooks integration
  • Communication history via our website forms or email

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used exclusively for:

  • Providing daily transaction recording and financial reporting
  • Communicating updates regarding your books
  • Responding to consultation requests

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or business information to third parties under any circumstances.

Data Security

As Intuit Certified ProAdvisors, we utilize industry-standard cloud accounting platforms (QuickBooks Online) that employ banking-level encryption. We follow zero-trust protocols for accessing client data and ensure all communication channels are secure.
